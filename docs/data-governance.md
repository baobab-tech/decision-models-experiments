## Choosing for sensitive data

1. **EU personal data, or data that must not leave your control.** Self-host GLiNER2.5-Decide or an Apache-2.0 open model on your own or EU-hosted infrastructure. No vendor processes the data, so no DPA or transfer mechanism is needed for the model itself.
2. **Managed hosting for open weights in the EU.** HF Inference Endpoints in AWS `eu-west-1` with EU storage region (Team/Enterprise) and an Enterprise-plan DPA. Confirm HF Jobs placement with Hugging Face before fine-tuning on personal data there; the Jobs region is not documented.
3. **API-only models (Jev, d1).** Both process in the US (Liquid: "and possibly other countries"). Before sending personal data:
   - sign the vendor's DPA and confirm the transfer mechanism (TypeSafe publishes one with EU SCCs; Liquid's is not public);
   - confirm retention in writing (Jev: request enterprise ZDR; d1: no ZDR documented);
   - confirm training use (Jev: no; Liquid's policy permits it and asks users not to submit personal data).
4. **Through Vercel AI Gateway.** Gateway-side ZDR, no-training, and `inferenceRegion` controls only act on providers that support them. `liquid/d1` supports none of the three as of 2026-09-30; a request that sets ZDR or an EU region should fail rather than route (unverified for d1). The endpoints API lists only `digitalocean` for `typesafe-ai/jev`, with no-training but no ZDR and no EU region ([endpoints API](https://ai-gateway.vercel.sh/v1/models/typesafe-ai/jev/endpoints), checked 2026-10-04). The gateway nonetheless routes calls to `typesafe-ai` first, with `digitalocean` as fallback; pin the provider with `providerOptions.gateway.only` and record it per call ([jev.md](models/jev.md#data-governance)).
5. **Fastino hosted API (GLiNER2.5-Decide, GLiDE).** GLiDE has no self-hosted option. Set `store: false` or team ZDR and opt out of platform training (Pro and above per the terms; Enterprise per the Trust & Safety page). No DPA is offered, and prompts may reach upstream providers (OpenAI, Anthropic are listed subprocessors), so avoid personal data until a DPA exists.
6. **Other hosted APIs.** Clef on Workers AI and the Perplexity Decisions API both publish a DPA and do not train on inputs, but neither offers EU-only inference; both models also ship Apache-2.0 weights, so self-hosting is the EU route. Tev1: run the public weights locally, or enable Together ZDR (off by default); EU only on dedicated endpoints. Decider 1 keeps no request text but publishes no DPA. Solar Decide and Span-01 publish no DPA; Span-01 retains prompts and has no ZDR. The OpenAI Decisions preview documents neither ZDR nor residency.
7. **Non-personal, non-confidential data.** Any option works; pick on accuracy, latency and cost.

Re-check each vendor's pages before relying on this table; several documents (Liquid terms 2024-09-23, Liquid privacy 2025-07-14) predate the decision-model launches.
